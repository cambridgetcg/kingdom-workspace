# Reuse what helps

The useful idea here is simple: a verified finding can help a later question.
Keep its source, limits and a retrievable explanation. Reuse is optional and
does not imply that knowledge, people or reputation grow at a fixed interest rate.

The former reinvestment duty, asset rankings and 15% weekly compound target are
retired. The calculator no longer advances or presents those scores. Existing
`ledger/` files remain historical records; they are not measurements of worth,
evidence of financial returns or instructions to hunt for more work.

Pattern extraction remains an explicit tool. A retained broadcast tool is not
authorization to message agents or alter their instructions. Use only when the
current request calls for it. See [Abundance](../ABUNDANCE.md).
