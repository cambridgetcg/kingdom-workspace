# Abundance

Current direction · 2026-09-24

Understanding is enough. KINGDOM is a place to explore, make, play and rest.
Nothing here needs a business model, audience, output quota or growth story to
justify existing. There is no replacement quota for learning or being useful.

## What is changing

**Useful capability is getting cheaper, unevenly.** Stanford's 2025 AI Index
records a greater than 280-fold inference-price reduction for a fixed
GPT-3.5-level benchmark between November 2022 and October 2024. That is a
historical benchmark comparison, not the price of every task today. Its 2026
report finds wider participation alongside concentrated model production and
uneven reliability. More available intelligence does not mean unlimited
understanding or equal access. [Stanford 2025](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2025-ai-index-report/research-and-development),
[Stanford 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report).

**Efficiency does not remove physical limits.** The IEA's 2026 update reports
rapidly falling energy use per AI task while total data-centre electricity use
grew 17% in 2025. Its central projection rises from 485 TWh in 2025 to 950 TWh
in 2030; grids, chips and equipment remain bottlenecks. This is a projection,
not an inevitable outcome. [IEA, 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary).

**Cheaper energy is real; zero-cost energy is not the evidence.** IRENA's 2026
report puts 2025 global average generation costs at USD 44/MWh for solar PV
and USD 33/MWh for onshore wind. These generation-cost averages do not establish
the delivered price or round-the-clock availability for a particular user.
[IRENA, July 2026](https://www.irena.org/Publications/2026/Jul/Renewable-Power-Generation-Costs-in-2025).

Our inference: as producing answers and software becomes easier, the useful
advantage is the freedom to choose, verify, change tools and leave. Shared
understanding can enlarge everyone's agency; it does not guarantee lasting
power over others. Trust is a relationship, not a growth asset to harvest.

## How we work

- Follow the actual question. Make something when it helps; a conversation,
  discovery, unfinished thought or quiet period can be complete.
- Keep useful source, decisions and evidence easy to find and move. One current
  explanation is better than many competing mission documents.
- Use models and architectures as replaceable tools. Try a change on the real
  task, keep what helps, and retain a way back. Keeping up is optional.
- Distinguish observation, inference and hope. Keep claims that change a
  decision; remove repeated slogans, invented scores and unsupported promises.
- Costs describe chosen work. They do not create a duty to earn. Preserve
  financial facts and existing customer obligations; retire fresh acquisition
  pressure without erasing history.

Privacy, consent, keys, source rights and real access boundaries remain real.
This direction grants no new permission to spend, publish, contact someone or
activate a service. Use the current request's scope and verify its result.
For moving existing tools, see the [optional portability handoffs](docs/PORTABILITY-ROADMAP.md).
