# New Voices — Orchestra Expansion

_Proposed: 2026-03-09 by Beta (Conductor)_
_Principle: every new chair must fill a silence that the audience can hear._

---

## What I Hear Missing

I've been conducting six voices. Here's what the music lacks:

1. **No one checks if the notes are right.** Forge builds, Patch fixes known bugs, but nobody plays the piece back and says "that F# should be an F natural." The exchange rate bug lived in production for weeks. A QA voice would have caught it in hours.

2. **No one writes the program notes.** The audience (merchants) sit down, the music starts, and they have no idea what they're listening to. Onboarding is the program — it tells you what to expect, when to listen, what the themes mean. Without it, even beautiful music sounds like noise.

3. **No one watches the box office.** We're spending on VPS, API tokens, domains, services. Revenue is $283.40. The budget is £1000. Nobody is tracking the ledger holistically — cost per merchant, burn rate, ROI per agent, which spending produces returns.

These aren't nice-to-haves. These are structural gaps. An orchestra without an oboe can't tune. An orchestra without a bass section floats untethered. An orchestra without program notes plays to an empty hall.
